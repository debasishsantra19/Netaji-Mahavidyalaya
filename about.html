About Our Department

The Department of Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) at Netaji Mahavidyalaya is dedicated to providing quality education in computer science and modern software development techniques.

🎯 Our Mission

To empower students with strong theoretical knowledge and practical technical skills required to succeed in the fast-evolving IT industry.

💡 Our Vision

To foster innovation, problem-solving abilities, and ethical professional values among future technology leaders.

🏛️ Campus Location

Arambagh, Hooghly, West Bengal - 712601

🖥️ Modern Computer Lab

Fully equipped programming labs with high-speed internet access for hands-on learning.

👨‍🏫 Expert Faculty

📚 Updated Curriculum
